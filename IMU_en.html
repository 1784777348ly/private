Introduction

MATRIXEVAL (the "App") is a utility application for connecting to and testing Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) hardware. Over Bluetooth, you can connect to an IMU device, receive inertial data such as acceleration and angular velocity, view data curves within the App, and save or export the data for your own analysis.

The App does not require registration or login. We do not collect your name, phone number, ID number, or any other personally identifiable information, and we do not upload your measurement data to any server.

The App is developed and operated by Changzhou Kunwei Sensor Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us" or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with applicable data protection laws. This Policy explains how the App handles information related to you.

Permissions

Bluetooth

Used to discover and connect to IMU hardware, receive acceleration and angular velocity data from the device, and send control commands such as start, stop and zero.

This permission is only used when you actively connect a device. If you decline, you cannot connect hardware, but the App can still launch and display existing records.

File Access

Used to export measurement data as CSV, Excel or ZIP files to a location you choose, and to import firmware update packages from the Files app.

Files are read or written only when you explicitly export or import. They are never uploaded to any server automatically.

Information We Collect

  • IMU measurement data: acceleration and angular velocity generated by the hardware device. This data is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any server.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: app version, device model, operating system version, crash stack traces and timestamps. Used solely to diagnose and fix defects.
  • Local configuration: sampling rate, language and privacy consent status, stored locally on your device.

We do NOT collect: name, phone number, ID number, location, contacts, photos, camera images, microphone recordings, health data or payment information. We do not use your information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Third-Party SDKs

Tencent Bugly (Crash Reporting)

Purpose: collects crash information and runtime exceptions to help the developer identify and fix defects, improving app stability.

Information that may be collected: device model, operating system version, app version, crash stack traces, crash time and device identifiers.

This SDK is initialized only after you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Privacy policy: Tencent Bugly SDK Privacy Policy

Other than the SDK listed above, the App does not integrate any other third-party SDK that collects personal information, and does not include advertising or analytics platforms.

How We Use Information

  • Display acceleration and angular velocity data in real time within the App
  • Store measurement data locally for your own export and analysis
  • Analyze crash logs to improve app stability
  • Remember your local preferences

Data Storage and Security

  • Measurement data and configuration are stored locally on your device
  • The App does not operate any server and does not transmit your measurement data to any third party
  • Crash information is transmitted to the SDK provider over encrypted channels
  • You may delete in-app records or uninstall the App to remove local data

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or otherwise provide your information to any third party, except:

  • With your explicit consent
  • As required by law, or in response to a mandatory request from judicial or administrative authorities

Your Rights

  • Disable permissions: you can turn off Bluetooth access for MATRIXEVAL in the system Settings at any time
  • Delete data: delete in-app records, or uninstall the App to remove all local data
  • Withdraw consent: uninstalling the App withdraws your consent; you will be asked to review and agree again on next use
  • Access and correction: local data can be viewed and managed directly within the App

Children's Privacy

The App is a device testing tool and is not directed to children under 14. Minors should use the App under the guidance of a guardian.
